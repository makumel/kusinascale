## 1. Brand Essence & Manifesto

**KusinaKita** is the Culinary & Negosyo Operating System for Filipino food entrepreneurs, carinderia owners, caterers, and home chefs. 

Unlike generic internet blogs or hallucinated AI recipes, **KusinaKita** is built on decades of real, sweat-tested kitchen experience from retired industry veterans and self-made fast food operators. Every recipe is calibrated for commercial portion scaling, and every dish is linked to a live costing calculator that guarantees a 30% to 70% net profit margin.

## 3. The 3 Origin Pillars (Credibility & Backstory)

### Pillar 1: The Retired Head Cook (25+ Years in Big Carinderia Chains)
* **The Contribution**: Commercial marination ratios, tenderizing tough cuts without burning excess LPG, bulk stock simmering, and the *“Tamang Sandok”* portioning standard to prevent profit loss.

### Pillar 2: The Self-Made Fast Food Entrepreneur
* **The Contribution**: The *“Diskarte”* pricing formula. Live wet market price synchronization, factoring in gas, cooking oil wear-and-tear, takeout plastic costs, and menu balancing (pairing low-margin meats with high-margin vegetables).

### Pillar 3: The Asian Street Food & Masterclass Vault
* **The Contribution**: Recipes acquired from paid culinary workshops and overseas street food masterclasses (Taiwan, Hong Kong, Japan, Thailand) translated for local palengke ingredients.

### Future Franchise Vault Categories:
* 🍕 **Franchise-Style Pizza & Pasta**: Secret cold-fermented dough and sweet-style sauce from a local pizza chain supervisor.
* 🍗 **Crispy Fried Chicken & Signature Gravy**: Commercial batter mix and thickened pan gravy that never separates.
* ☕ **Commercial Palamig & Coffee**: High-margin (75–85%) beverage bases, syrups, and street food thirst-quenchers.
